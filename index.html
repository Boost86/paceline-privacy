Paceline Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026

Paceline is a teleprompter for Android. It is built so that what you write, say and record stays on your phone. This policy explains exactly what the app does with your information, which is very little.

The short version

  • Paceline does not collect, store on a server, sell or share your personal information.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app has no permission to access the internet. Android itself stops it from sending anything anywhere.

Your scripts

Scripts you write or paste are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. They are not uploaded anywhere, and Paceline turns off Android's automatic app backup so they are not copied to your Google account either. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Camera and microphone

Paceline uses the camera and microphone only while you are recording or using Smart Scroll, and only after you grant permission. Video and audio are processed on your phone. Recordings are kept in the app's private storage until you choose to save one, which puts it in your phone's Movies/Paceline folder. Caption files you save go to Download/Paceline.

Smart Scroll (voice following)

Smart Scroll listens to your voice so the script can follow you. It uses Android's on-device speech recognition: the audio is handed to the speech service on your phone and is never sent over the internet by Paceline. If your script is in a language whose speech model is not on your phone yet, Paceline can ask Android to download it. That download is made by Android's speech service, the same way as when you add a language in Settings.

Purchases

Paceline Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment. Paceline only receives confirmation from Google Play, on your phone, of whether you own Pro. Payment details never reach the app. Google's handling of purchases is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Camera: to record video while you read.
  • Microphone: to record sound, and to follow your voice with Smart Scroll.
  • Google Play billing: to buy and restore Paceline Pro.

Children

Paceline is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. If Paceline ever starts handling your information differently, the app will tell you before it does.
